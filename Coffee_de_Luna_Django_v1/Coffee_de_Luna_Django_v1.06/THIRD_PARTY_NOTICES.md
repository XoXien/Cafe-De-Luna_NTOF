# Assets and dependencies

- **DM Sans** and **Fraunces** are bundled from Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font License texts are included in `static/fonts/`. Font sources: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans and https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces.
- The login background coffee photograph is sourced from Unsplash, image identifier `photo-1611564494260-6f21b80af7ea`. Source: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1611564494260-6f21b80af7ea . License: https://unsplash.com/license . It is bundled for use as a website background.
- The Coffee de Luna identity and café SVG illustrations were created for this project.
- Django is distributed under the BSD 3-Clause license: https://github.com/django/django/blob/main/LICENSE . Pillow license information: https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow/blob/main/LICENSE . Dependencies are installed separately through `requirements.txt`.
- Automated tests use temporary fictional records in isolated test databases. No preset staff accounts or sample orders are packaged.

- The reference Coffee de Luna menu image was supplied by the user for this project. Menu prices and offered sizes were transcribed from that image. Ingredient forms, recipes, unit costs, and opening quantities are setup assumptions.
